

Creating vision documents is a helpful way for people to get aligned on what to build, and also serve as the way for agents to always be grounded against the things that you want to build.
Throughout creating these, our own understanding of the problem might be skewed, wrong, or misaligned with what is possible. ReVisioner aims to automatically:
1. Find risky assumptions
2. For each of them, research and/or test (do a spike). 
3. For each tested assumption in step 2, have data that supports it being resolved, needs unblocking, or is unknownable (with suggestions on how to make it knowable)
